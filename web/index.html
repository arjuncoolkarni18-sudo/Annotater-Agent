A little guide to Margin.

  1. Open your PDF. Use your own document or explore the sample.
  2. Pick a style. Digital text or handwriting. Start with the sample style, or draw your own letters.
  3. Add a thought. Write a note or take an excerpt from the page. Review any suggested wording.
  4. Make your mark. Click Add to the page, then choose a spot. Select a note to edit, drag it to move, or use its × to delete. Drag with Highlight to mark an area.
  5. Keep a copy. Export adds the visible notes and highlights to a copy of your PDF.
